## Overview
HiringRadar is an automated, real-time SDE matching and alert engine designed specifically for college graduates and freshers. It aggregates corporate job postings, normalizes criteria, evaluates eligibility (fresher vs. senior, VLSI vs. CS background), performs semantic matching using local vector embeddings, and delivers high-relevance target listings straight to candidate Telegram channels.
